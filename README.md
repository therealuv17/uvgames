# 🏎️ Race Car Game

A fun and exciting race car game built with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript!

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Click "Start Game" to begin
3. Use **Arrow Keys** or **WASD** to control your car:
   - **Left/Right** or **A/D**: Move sideways
   - **Up/Down** or **W/S**: Move forward/backward
4. Avoid the purple obstacles coming toward you
5. Score points by avoiding obstacles
6. The game gets faster as your score increases!

## Features

- Smooth car controls
- Scrolling road animation
- Dynamic difficulty (speed increases over time)
- Score tracking
- Game over screen with restart option
- Beautiful modern UI

## Game Mechanics

- Your car is red
- Obstacles are purple
- Stay on the road (between the yellow lines)
- Each obstacle avoided = 10 points
- Game speed increases gradually

Enjoy racing! 🏁
